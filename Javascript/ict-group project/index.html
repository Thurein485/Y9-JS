Spring

A time of renewal and growth when temperatures warm, snow melts, and trees sprout fresh leaves alongside blooming flowers.

Summer

The warmest period of the year, characterized by long, sunny days, lush foliage, and high outdoor activity.

Monsoon

Defined by shifting seasonal winds that bring torrential rainfall, replenishing rivers and revitalizing ecosystems.

Autumn

A cooling transition phase where daylight shrinks and deciduous trees shed their leaves in vibrant hues of red, orange, and yellow.

Winter

The coldest season, marked by short days, freezing temperatures, dormant plant life, and widespread snow or frost.
